## TL;DR (the ten decisions that matter)

1. **Account type:** Business account (free switch) with original voice-over plus Commercial Music Library (CML) audio only. Trending sounds are off-limits in-app for Business accounts [S6][S7][S8].
2. **AI labelling is not optional:** turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" toggle on every video, add a persistent on-screen "AI host" tag, and say it in the caption/bio [S2][S12].
3. **Turn on "Your brand" (Disclose commercial content) on every video.** The Sept 2026 guidelines say promoting "a product, brand, or business" requires the content disclosure setting [S12][S13].
4. **Max 5 hashtags.** One broad audience tag, two topical mid/niche tags, one seasonal/cohort tag, one series/long-tail tag. No #fyp/#viral [S39][S41].
5. **Keywords live in the voice-over, the first on-screen text, and the first line of the caption**, not in the hashtags (hashtags are a weak, capped signal) [S24][S34][S25].
6. **No testimonials, no usage numbers, no first-person experience claims by Norah.** Use named, dated third-party statistics only [S59][S61][S62].
7. **Series format** (numbered Part n/5, tease the next part, video replies to comments) is the best-evidenced follower lever for small accounts [S26][S29].
8. **Post slots:** 12:30 ET and 19:30 ET (Paris 18:30 and 01:30 next day; shift by one hour during 25–31 Oct, see section 4).
9. **Do not reuse identical clips.** Re-edit B-roll, new script and new first 3 seconds each time (section 5.6).
10. **Realism verdict:** 100 followers in 15 days is a stretch but plausible target, not the expected outcome. Plan for 20–150, central case roughly 35–100 (section 6).

## 1. HASHTAGS

### 1.1 What is actually known (Oct 2026)

- **Cap:** TikTok began telling some users "five hashtags max" in mid-Aug 2025 without an official explanation [S39]; Planoly confirmed it as a cap in Nov 2025 [S40]; Dash Social (updated 8 Oct 2026) states TikTok caps posts at five and suggests 3–5 [S41]. Grade B/C.
- **Role in ranking:** TikTok lists hashtags with captions and sounds as "video information" used by the recommender [S1] (grade A, 2020). Hootsuite (1 June 2026) says keyword-rich captions, voice-overs and on-screen text now carry equal or greater weight for discoverability [S24].
- **Measured effect is small and unproven:** one observational study of 1,569 videos from 40 public accounts (mostly large) found a median normalised score of 1.012 with hashtags vs 0.975 without, about 3.7%; the author calls it correlational [S43]. Grade C.
- **Competition rule of thumb:** Dash Social suggests aiming for tags with fewer than 1 million videos for less competition [S41].
- **Wedding tags are evergreen, not trending:** the TikTok Creative Center "trending hashtags" lists reproduced by Dash Social on 7 Oct 2026 (US, last 30 days) contain no wedding tag [S41].
- **Cohort labelling is a real behaviour:** wedding creators label themselves by wedding year ("2026 bride", "2028 bride") and use hooks like "things I've already done for my wedding" [S58]. The "2027 bride" tag is almost empty today (see table), so say "2027 bride" aloud and in on-screen text, not only in a hashtag.

**Rotation rules (grade D, my design):**

- Consecutive posts share at most two tags; no identical five-tag set on consecutive days.
- Each non-audience tag is used at most about six times across the 30 videos.
- Keep a log: tags, 24h views, 72h views, search views (Creator Search Insights, section 2). Review at Day 8; swap the weakest third. With 30 posts the sample is tiny, so use it for sanity, not statistics.
- Caption: first sentence contains the exact search phrase (section 2); hashtags go last.

## 2. TIKTOK SEARCH / SEO

### 2.1 How search and suggestions work (what is known and not known)

| Point | What the sources say | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs the recommender officially uses | "Video information" = captions, sounds, hashtags, alongside user interactions and device/account settings [S1] | A (2020) |

| Practitioner test | A 2022 test found three keyword-targeted videos ranking in the top four for the author's own searches (n=3, one account, 2022) [S32] | C |
| Keyword research tool | "Creator Search Insights" (search for it in the app): Search analytics (search views, average search ranking), Suggested (All, Content gap, Searches by followers) and Trending. | Search-as-question test | TikTok tested a "Post your question" prompt in search on iOS (July 2025) [S68]. | New-account rule | No follower minimum for appearing in search is documented. TikTok says follower count is not a direct recommendation factor [S1] (for search, **unverified**) | A for recommendations, D for search |

**Implication for a new account (grade D, inference):** target long-tail question phrases where few videos exist (the "Content gap" idea), say the exact phrase early, repeat it in on-screen text and the caption's first line, and use video replies to seed the same phrase in comments (comments feed search suggestions per [S34]).

**Profile strings (character counts checked):**

- Name field (30-character limit [S35]), options with character counts checked: `Wedillybird | Wedding RSVP` (26) or, carrying the disclosure, `Norah (AI) | Wedding Invites` (28).
- Bio (67 chars): `AI host Norah: wedding invites, RSVPs & guest lists. By Wedillybird`
- Do **not** write "wedding planner" or "wedding expert" for Norah: it asserts a human credential she does not have (section 5.4).

### 5.2 Business-account music

| Scenario | Total views (30 videos) | Conversion | Followers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low: ~150 views per video | 4,500 | 0.1–0.3% | about 5–15 |
| Central: ~400 views per video | 12,000 | 0.3–0.6% | about 35–70 |
| Buffer-style median (~500 views per video) plus 3 modest hits (5–10K each) | ~37,500 | 0.3–0.7% | about 110–260 |
| One breakout (50K) on top of central | ~62,000 | 0.3–0.6% on the rest, 0.5–1.0% on the breakout | about 290–570 |

Views needed for 100 followers: 10,000 at 1.0% conversion, 20,000 at 0.5%, 33,000 at 0.3%, 100,000 at 0.1%.

### 6.3 Verdict (plain)

- **100 followers in 15 days is a stretch target, plausible but not the expected outcome.** Central expectation: roughly 10–15K total views and 35–100 followers; planning range 20–150. Hitting 100 usually requires at least one video at roughly 10x the typical median (5–10K+ views) that converts at 0.5% or better, which in the evidence means a niche-aligned video with a clear "follow for the series" reason. I judge ≥100 as less likely than not (judgement; no dataset supports a probability).
- **Why your odds may be lower than the case studies:** (1) faceless AI host and the AI label (viewers can dial AI content down [S2][S44]); (2) Planoly and Sadler both found personal, recurring, story-like content converts better than polished or trend content [S29][S31]; (3) 0 followers and 0 social proof; (4) 75–100 s lengths are unproven for follower growth; (5) possible duplicate-content suppression if clips are reused.
- **Why they may be higher:** intent-heavy search topics, an underserved long-tail (guest list, RSVP, website, budget), a series format, real product screens as proof, and answering comments with video replies.
- **Be careful with off-niche virality:** a 160K-view Eras Tour video brought 36 followers (0.02%) [S28]. A viral video that does not match the profile promise adds views, not followers.
- **Two weeks is also short for compounding:** videos keep earning views for months [S32][S30]; judge the account on Day 30–60, not Day 15.

## 7. Operating rules for the 15 days (compact)

1. One pillar and one target phrase per video; phrase in the first sentence, first on-screen text and first caption line.
2. Hook in 2–3 s; open loop by 15 s; last line leads back to the first; hold the last frame 1–2 s.
3. Series: "Part n/5" on screen, next-part tease in the last 3 s, pinned comment with phrase and next part.
4. Five hashtags per section 1.3; log them.
5. Disclosure stack on every video (section 5.5) plus "Your brand" toggle.
6. Audio: original voice-over + CML only; no stitches in the first 15 days unless silent.
7. Daily: 2–3 video replies; 10–15 genuine niche comments (heuristic); no links, bots or pods.
8. Day 3, 5, 8, 10 reviews: median 24 h views, completion, shares per 1,000 views, search views, profile views → follows. If median 24 h views are under about 150 by Day 5 (my threshold, **unverified**), change hooks and topics, not hashtags. Benchmarks to beat: Buffer median ~460–510 views/post, Socialinsider 1–5K-account mean 350.
9. Make 6–8 videos 45 s or shorter as a length control.
10. Re-benchmark every 5 days; ignore any claimed "best practice" you cannot see in your own analytics.

## 8. Day-0 verification checklist (things I could not confirm)

1. Account type chosen; Verified Business application started if you want a link in bio.
2. Private test upload on the web uploader: is the AI-generated content toggle present? Is "Disclose commercial content → Your brand" present? Does the scheduler date picker allow 10 days or 30? Does a scheduled post keep both toggles?
3. CML: shortlist 6–8 tracks; confirm none is a fee-bearing "Premium Track".
4. Search every planned hashtag and phrase in-app; record suggestions; open Creator Search Insights → Content gap for the Stage C phrases.
5. Bio limit (80 vs 160) and name field (30).
6. Read the live Community Guidelines (effective 24 Sept 2026) on AIGC, commercial disclosure, unoriginal content and For You eligibility.
7. Licence check for voice, video and music generators (commercial use, attribution).
8. Demo data in every screen capture (no real guest names/phones); QR codes mocked.
9. Counsel check on NY synthetic-performer law and EU AI Act Art. 50 scope for organic brand posts.
